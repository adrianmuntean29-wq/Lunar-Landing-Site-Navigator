What is simulated here and what the competition version would use

Terrain
Real: NASA LOLA heights (PGDA product 90, LDEM_80S_80MPP_ADJ) averaged to 240 m by our Python engine (data/heights.bin), shown two ways: "All sites", a 230 × 230 km map from the engine's 640 m grid (data/region_heights.bin) that holds every site in data/sites.json, and "Close-up of the pole", a 48 × 48 km map at 240 m. Shadows also use 46 km of terrain beyond each map edge. Site pins sit at the published coordinates; a site outside the map is pinned at its edge with its distance. If the heights file cannot load, the page falls back to a generated demo terrain and says so.
Sun and Earth
Real: one JPL Horizons table (2026-2030, every 6 h) of the sub-solar and sub-Earth points in the Moon's mean-Earth frame. The page interpolates it and computes the Sun and Earth direction from any site, including Earth parallax. Checked against Horizons topocentric values.
Computation
3D demo: each point's horizon in 64 directions out to 46 km, with the Moon's curvature. Real sites: 360-direction horizon masks from LOLA terrain, computed by our Python engine (flat horizon until they load).
