What is simulated here and what the competition version would use

Terrain
Generated: a 21 km crater near the pole and a massif toward Earth, on a 48 × 48 km map. The real version would use LOLA elevation models (Moon Trek, PDS).
Sun and Earth
Simplified motion: the Sun makes a full circle every 29.5 days and rises or sinks by ±1.54° over a year; Earth wobbles ±6.7° vertically and ±7.9° horizontally. The real version would use NASA SPICE ephemerides.
Computation
Each point's horizon is computed in 64 directions (360 for candidate sites), out to 46 km, including the Moon's curvature. The same computation works on real data.
